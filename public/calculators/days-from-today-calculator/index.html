Guide: Days From Today Calculator

What it does

Adds or subtracts a whole number of calendar days from today's date.

How to use it

Enter a positive number for a future date or a negative number for a past date. Quick buttons fill common offsets.

Formula

Result date = today's local date + entered calendar days.

Example

Entering 30 shows the calendar date 30 days from today. Entering -7 shows the date one week ago.

FAQs

Can I use negative days?

Yes. Negative values subtract days from today and return a past date.

Does this count calendar days or business days?

This calculator uses calendar days. It does not skip weekends or holidays.

Does the result depend on time zone?

The calculator uses the calendar date in your browser's local time zone.
