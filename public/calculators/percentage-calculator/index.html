Guide: Percentage Calculator

What it does

Handles everyday percentage math for classwork, discounts, marks, pricing, and quick comparisons.

How to use it

Choose the calculation type, enter the two values, and the result updates immediately.

Formula

p% of n = (p / 100) x n; percent change = (new - old) / old x 100.

Example

20% of 50 is 10. If 80 increases to 100, the percentage change is 25%.

FAQs

How do I calculate a percentage of a number?

Multiply the number by the percentage divided by 100. For example, 20% of 50 is 50 x 0.20 = 10.

Why is percentage change negative?

Percentage change is negative when the new value is lower than the original value.

Should I round percentage answers?

Round only for display or school instructions. Keep full precision while doing intermediate calculations.
