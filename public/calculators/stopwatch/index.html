    Guide: Stopwatch

    What it does

    Measures elapsed time in the browser and stores lap rows while the page stays open.

    How to use it

    Press Start, use Lap to save a split time, Pause when needed, and Reset to clear the timer.

    Formula

    Elapsed time = current browser timestamp - start timestamp + saved elapsed time.

    Example

    Use laps to time exercise sets, cooking stages, study sprints, or simple work intervals.

    FAQs

    Does the stopwatch keep running if I close the page?

    No. The stopwatch runs in your current browser tab and resets if the page is closed or reloaded.

    Are lap times sent to a server?

    No. Lap times are kept in the browser page only unless you copy them yourself.

    How precise is the stopwatch?

    It is suitable for everyday timing. Browser scheduling, device sleep, and heavy tabs can affect precision.
