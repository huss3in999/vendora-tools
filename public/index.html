Frequently Asked Questions

Common Questions Before Booking

Is Vendora Transport a standard local street taxi?

No. Vendora Transport is a private transport company offering dedicated chauffeur services, private car with driver, airport transfers, and cross-border GCC transit with pre-confirmed vehicles and drivers.

Is the price per passenger or per vehicle?

Published pricing and quotes are for the complete private vehicle within its confirmed capacity, not per individual passenger.

What payment methods are accepted?

We accept Cash and BenefitPay. Payment terms and deposit requirements (if applicable) are confirmed before booking finalization.

How are driver and vehicle details confirmed?

Once your itinerary is confirmed via WhatsApp, our dispatch operations team sends your assigned driver's contact info and vehicle category before pickup time.

What is the cancellation and refund policy?

Bookings without a deposit may generally be cancelled without financial penalty (please notify us at least 2 hours before pickup for short-notice bookings). Deposits are refundable within agreed terms prior to dispatch. If Vendora cannot fulfill a confirmed booking and no acceptable alternative is agreed, payments received for that booking are refunded.
