GetVendora Platform

About GetVendora

GetVendora (Vendora) is a business platform serving Bahrain and the GCC with three connected offerings: private GCC transport booking, free business tools, and restaurant POS solutions — all under one brand at getvendora.net.

Three Core Services

Vendora is not just a calculator site or a transport listing — it is one entity delivering transport, tools, and restaurant operations support.

GCC Transport Services

Private door-to-door passenger and parcel transport between Bahrain, Saudi Arabia, Kuwait, Qatar, UAE, and Oman. Book 24/7 via WhatsApp on the Vendora Transport hub.

Business Tools & Calculators

Free online tools for commissions, margins, invoices, PDF conversion, and small-business finance. Explore the tools hub or searchable all-tools directory.

Restaurant & POS Solutions

Restaurant-first tools including food cost calculators, QR menus, invoice generators, and POS workflows. See restaurant calculators and Vendora Pro POS.

What Vendora Does

Beyond free tools, Vendora connects operational software with real-world GCC transport services for businesses and travelers in the region.

Restaurant management system

Vendora Pro connects POS and operations with simpler day-to-day tools that restaurant owners can actually use — pricing, QR ordering, invoicing, and margins.

Tools that solve real tasks

The platform includes a QR menu generator, invoice generator, food cost calculator, and menu price calculator.

Who Vendora Is Built For

Vendora serves both regional transport customers and food-business operators — two audiences united by the GetVendora platform brand.

  • Travelers and businesses needing private GCC transport between Bahrain, Saudi Arabia, and Gulf destinations.
  • Small restaurants, cafes, food trucks, takeaway shops, cloud kitchens, and catering businesses.
  • Small business owners who need free calculators for margins, commissions, invoices, and daily operations.
  • Bakeries and juice shops that want restaurant-style tools without extra complexity.
