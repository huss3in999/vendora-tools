About Our Company

Professional Private Transport & Chauffeur Services

Vendora Transport is a real Bahrain-based private transport company headquartered in Seef. We specialize in dedicated car with driver services, airport transfers, and cross-border GCC passenger transport.

  • Headquartered in Seef, Kingdom of Bahrain

  • Serving families, corporate executives, tourists, and airport passengers
Our Business Model

Owned Fleet & Approved Network Partners

Vendora Transport owns and operates its core fleet of company vehicles. To maintain seamless reliability during peak seasons, high demand, or specialized route requirements, we cooperate with approved transport offices and trusted partners across Bahrain and the GCC.

Before any trip begins, our dispatch team confirms the exact assigned vehicle category and driver contact information to the customer, ensuring complete transparency and safety.

Driver Excellence

Experienced GCC Drivers

Our drivers are thoroughly familiar with Bahrain city routes, King Fahd Causeway procedures, regional airports (BAH, DMM, KWI, DOH, DXB), luxury hotels, business towers, and shopping destinations.

Whether navigating border procedures or coordinating timely airport pickups, our team is dedicated to a smooth, comfortable journey.
