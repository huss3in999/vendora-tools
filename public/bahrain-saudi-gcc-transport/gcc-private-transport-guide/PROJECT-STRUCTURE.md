# Project Structure

## Source

- `src/ar/` - future Arabic page source and Arabic-only components.
- `src/en/` - future English page source and English-only components.
- `src/shared/` - shared planning notes for deterministic route planner data, component names, and non-copy logic.

## Research

- `research/incoming/` - raw research reports exactly as provided.
- `research/processed/` - distilled findings, extracted keywords, route lists, FAQs, and conclusions.
- `research/competitors/` - competitor page notes, SERP observations, offer comparison, and trust/CRO ideas.
- `research/search-intent/` - query intent clusters, user problems, and page-to-intent mapping.

## SEO

- `seo/keywords/` - keyword database and language-specific query groups.
- `seo/schema/` - JSON-LD planning, schema rules, and validation notes.
- `seo/metadata/` - SEO title, meta description, OG/Twitter, canonical, hreflang, and robots planning.

## Content

- `content/sections/` - section-by-section content requirements.
- `content/drafts/` - Arabic and English copy drafts before implementation.
- `content/faqs/` - FAQ database and grouped FAQ sets.

## Data

- `data/routes/` - route data, city/airport lists, estimated durations, and internal links.
- `data/pricing/` - future pricing table and quote-factor placeholders.
- `data/reviews/` - future verified Google review exports and review-use rules.
- `data/company-documents/` - future company documents, policies, proof, and operational notes.

## Assets

- `assets/images/real-vehicle-photos/` - future real fleet, interior, pickup, luggage, and driver-neutral photos.
- `assets/images/generated/` - generated or edited production images.
- `assets/images/prompts/` - image prompt library and image generation notes.
- `assets/maps/` - route maps, SVG/PNG/WebP map assets, and map source notes.
- `assets/icons/` - future custom icons if current Lucide icons are not enough.

## Planning And QA

- `planning/` - blueprints, decisions, build plan, launch plan, and open questions.
- `qa/` - future audits, screenshots, Playwright reports, language checks, and schema validation notes.
