# GCC Private Transport Guide

Isolated planning and development workspace for the future GetVendora Transport flagship authority page.

This folder is not a live route and should not be linked, deployed, or added to sitemaps until the page is approved for production.

## Purpose

Build a long-term master authority page for GCC private transport covering Bahrain, Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Kuwait, UAE, Oman, Iraq, airport transfers, route planning, WhatsApp booking, family travel, business/VIP travel, parcel/document delivery, trust/safety, pricing placeholders, and mega FAQs.

## Guardrails

- Do not modify existing live transport pages from this workspace.
- Do not publish fixed prices until approved pricing data exists.
- Do not add fake reviews, fake licenses, or unsupported company claims.
- Keep Arabic and English page plans separate and language-pure.
- Treat all research and uploaded files as source material, not direct public copy.

## Key Folders

- `src/` - future page source drafts and implementation work.
- `research/` - incoming and processed research reports.
- `seo/` - keyword, metadata, and schema planning.
- `content/` - section outlines, copy drafts, and FAQ database.
- `data/` - structured route, pricing, review, and company data.
- `assets/` - images, generated visuals, maps, icons, and prompts.
- `references/` - current-site references, competitor notes, and external source summaries.
- `planning/` - implementation blueprints, decisions, and release planning.
- `qa/` - future QA checklists, audit notes, screenshots, and validation results.
