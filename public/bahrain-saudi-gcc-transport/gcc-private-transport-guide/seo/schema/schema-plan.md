# Schema Plan

Status: placeholder.

Allowed schema candidates:

- `WebPage`
- `BreadcrumbList`
- `Service` or `TaxiService`
- `FAQPage`
- `ItemList`
- `HowTo` for booking steps, if the visible page supports it

Do not use:

- `AggregateRating` without real visible reviews on the page
- fake license, award, or certification claims
- fixed price `Offer` markup until approved pricing exists
