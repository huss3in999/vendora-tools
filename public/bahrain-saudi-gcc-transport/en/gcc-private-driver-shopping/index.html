Private Driver vs. Normal Street Taxi

Understand why travelers choose a dedicated private driver for GCC journeys.

Dedicated Private Driver

  • Vehicle and driver arranged specifically around your itinerary
  • Vehicle remains with you across multiple stops and shopping destinations
  • Purchases and luggage remain securely inside the car
  • Pre-confirmed vehicle category and transparent custom quotation
  • Continuous WhatsApp operations communication throughout the journey

Standard Point-to-Point Taxi

  • Single drop-off only; car leaves immediately upon arrival
  • Must hail a new unknown taxi for every subsequent destination
  • Must carry shopping bags and luggage into every mall and shop
  • Unpredictable vehicle condition and ticking meter anxiety
  • No advance multi-day or multi-stop coordination
