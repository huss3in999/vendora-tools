Tolls and border documents

King Fahd Causeway: confirm the current vehicle toll in the JESR app or with the authority. The former BHD 2.500 / SAR 25 tariff was superseded in February 2026. Ask whether the confirmed fare includes tolls, insurance and waiting.

GCC citizens: carry an original passport or a national ID accepted for this crossing. Residents: carry your original passport, valid residence permit and the destination or transit visas required for your nationality. Check exit/re-entry requirements with your residence country. Declare restricted goods to customs; the driver cannot guarantee entry.
