Tolls and border documents

King Fahd Causeway: confirm the current vehicle toll in the JESR app or with the authority. The former BHD 2.500 / SAR 25 tariff was superseded in February 2026. Ask whether the confirmed fare includes tolls, insurance and waiting.

GCC citizens: carry an original passport or a national ID accepted for this crossing. Residents: carry your original passport, valid residence permit and the destination or transit visas required for your nationality. Check exit/re-entry requirements with your residence country. Questions for this route

How long is the Kuwait International Airport to Bahrain road journey?

Allow approximately 5.5–7 driving hours over 430–520 km. King Fahd Causeway processing, customs inspection, traffic and rest stops add time. Confirm exact pickup and drop-off addresses before departure.

Where can I be dropped off in Bahrain?

Manama hotels, Seef business district, Juffair, and Bahrain International Airport. Share the building entrance, hotel lobby, or flight arrival details for pickup.
