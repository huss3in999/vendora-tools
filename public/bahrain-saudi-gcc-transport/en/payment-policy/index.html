Payment Policy & Accepted Methods

This page details our confirmed payment options, vehicle pricing structure, and toll inclusions.

1. Accepted Payment Methods

Vendora Transport accepts the following confirmed payment methods:

  • Cash: Payable directly to the driver or operations representative per booking instructions.
  • BenefitPay: Direct electronic bank transfer via the BenefitPay app in Bahrain.

Note: Direct online card payment is a future possibility currently under consideration.

2. Pricing Vehicle-Based Structure

All quotes and published rates represent the cost for the entire private vehicle up to its confirmed capacity, not per individual passenger.

3. Tolls & Causeway Inclusion

Cross-border quotes include standard route charges and King Fahd Causeway tolls where applicable, unless explicitly noted otherwise during booking confirmation.
