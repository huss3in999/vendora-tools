What is Vendora Transport?

Vendora Transport is a Bahrain-based private transport and delivery booking service for Bahrain, Saudi Arabia, and GCC routes. Customers can request private trips, airport pickup and drop-off, passenger transport, parcel delivery, and custom item delivery through WhatsApp, depending on availability and trip details.

How WhatsApp booking requests work

The fastest way to confirm availability and pricing is a complete message.

Pick a route page

Start with a route that matches your destination (Khobar, Dammam, Riyadh, Kuwait, Qatar, UAE, Dubai, Oman, and more).

Send trip details

Include pickup point, destination area, date/time, passenger count, and luggage notes.

Request a quote

Pricing depends on destination, timing, passengers, luggage, and whether the trip is one-way or return.

Parcels (optional)

If you need a parcel delivery, include the item type and size so it can be checked against regulations and safe capacity.
