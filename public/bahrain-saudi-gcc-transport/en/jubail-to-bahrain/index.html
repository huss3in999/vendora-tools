What is the price of a private taxi from Jubail to Bahrain?

The standard fixed rate is Price on request per complete vehicle one way, including King Fahd Causeway tolls. The fare covers the entire vehicle, not per passenger.

Can I book a transfer from Jubail directly to Bahrain Airport (BAH)?

Yes. We drop off directly at the Bahrain Airport departures terminal. We advise scheduling pickup at least 4 to 5 hours prior to your flight departure time.

No. You travel smoothly in the same vehicle and with the same driver from Jubail all the way to Bahrain.
