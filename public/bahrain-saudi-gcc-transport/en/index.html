Customer questions

Frequently asked questions

Is the price per passenger or for the complete vehicle?

Published public prices are for the complete vehicle within confirmed capacity unless stated otherwise.

Do you provide Bahrain Airport pickup?

Yes. Send the flight, date, time, addresses, passengers and luggage for confirmation.

Can you take me from the airport to a hotel?

Yes. Share the hotel and arrival details so the meeting point can be confirmed.

Can I book a private car for a full day?

Yes. Send the requested hours and expected stops for package and price confirmation.

Do you provide transport inside Bahrain?

Yes, including airports, hotels, appointments, malls, restaurants and private city journeys.

Do you travel from Bahrain to Saudi Arabia?

Yes. Journeys via King Fahd Causeway to Khobar, Dammam, Riyadh and other destinations can be requested.

Can you arrange a large SUV for families and luggage?

Yes, subject to passenger count, bag sizes and availability. Send these details before choosing a vehicle.

Can you arrange more than one vehicle?

Multiple vehicles can be requested for groups and are confirmed with availability and price.

Are child seats available on request?

They may be requested in advance with the children's ages, subject to availability and confirmation.

What information is required for an airport booking?

Flight number, date, time, airport, pickup and destination, passengers, children and luggage.

What happens if a flight is delayed?

Send the delay update through WhatsApp. Waiting or flight monitoring applies only as confirmed for the booking.

Can I book a return journey?

Yes. Include the return date and time so both directions can be priced and confirmed.

Which payment methods are available?

The accepted method is stated during booking confirmation. Do not assume a method before operations confirms it.

How early should I book?

Earlier is better for airports, families and cross-border travel. Urgent requests remain subject to availability.

Is the exact vehicle model guaranteed?

No. Images are illustrative; the vehicle type and model depend on journey details and availability at confirmation.
