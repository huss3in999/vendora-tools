King Fahd Causeway transport guide

Private transport between Bahrain and Saudi Arabia uses King Fahd Causeway. Journey time varies with border traffic, document checks, weekends, and holiday periods.

Before you travel

  • Confirm that every passenger has the required passport, visa, and entry documents.
  • Allow extra time for immigration and customs, especially during peak periods.
  • Share your pickup point, destination, passenger count, luggage, and preferred travel time when requesting availability.

Border approval always remains the responsibility of the relevant authorities. GetVendora provides transport coordination and does not guarantee entry.
