How airport confirmation works

Flight timing

The terminal and meeting point must be agreed during confirmation; no universal meeting point is promised.

Delays and waiting

Send delay updates on WhatsApp. Flight monitoring and included waiting apply only if explicitly confirmed.

Passenger and luggage capacity, GMC/XL requests, child seats and multiple vehicles are confirmed before travel.
