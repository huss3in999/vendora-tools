Customer Rights & Fair Review Guarantees at Vendora Transport

Every customer and passenger has the right to raise concerns, ask questions, or file a formal complaint regarding service quality, driver conduct, vehicle cleanliness, delays, or payment details:

  • All complaints are treated with absolute confidentiality and reviewed directly by Vendora management.
  • Filing a complaint is your right and does not affect your right to future transport bookings.
  • We welcome constructive feedback to continually elevate our private transport experience.
  • For urgent matters during active journeys, contact our support desk directly via call or WhatsApp: +973 3322 5954.
