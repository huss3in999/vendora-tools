Plan the Bahrain to DMM journey

Causeway traffic, passport and customs processing, Bahrain pickup location and airline check-in time can all affect the required departure time. Allow a generous margin and verify the flight directly with the airline.

Information to send

Pickup address, travel date, desired pickup time, airline, flight number, departure time, passengers, children and luggage.

Vehicle and luggage

Capacity may reach seven passengers, but seven people with many bags may require a different vehicle or two vehicles. GMC/XL requests depend on availability.

Children and child seats

Families can request transport. State every child’s age and request any child seat during confirmation; it is not assumed or guaranteed.

Return and waiting

A return journey, extra stop or waiting arrangement can be requested separately. Hours, waiting and extra charges must be confirmed before travel.

Airport and Causeway guidance

King Fahd Causeway congestion can change journey time. Travel and entry rules may change, and Vendora Transport does not guarantee border entry. Verify your current eligibility and documents through the Bahrain National Portal, King Fahd Causeway Authority and the official Saudi visa portal. Check flight status through King Fahd International Airport or your airline.
