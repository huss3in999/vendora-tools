Frequently Asked Questions

Key details before booking your journey.

What is the price of a private taxi from Bahrain to Jubail?

The standard fixed price is 70 BHD (~700 SAR) per complete vehicle one way, with all King Fahd Causeway tolls included. The charge is per car, not per seat.

How long does the drive to Jubail Industrial City take?

The journey typically takes 1.5 to 2 hours (approx. 150 km) via King Fahd Causeway and Route 613, depending on border traffic and your exact drop-off area in Jubail.

Is this service suitable for corporate and industrial travel?

Yes. It is suitable for business visitors, engineers, and corporate travel to Jubail industrial areas and Royal Commission districts.

Can urgent documents or small commercial parts be transported?

Yes. We offer secure hand-carry parcel and document delivery between Bahrain and Jubail facilities.
